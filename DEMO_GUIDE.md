# Rumi demo guide

A step-by-step walkthrough for anyone presenting or trying Rumi.

**Updated: October 29, 2026.** This guide covers the simplified **Rumi iPhone** app and its matching **Web** experience, not the older app screens. Allow about **15 minutes for Steps 1–7**. Voice, recipe research and the care story are optional extras.

## Before you begin

- Open the Rumi iPhone app or the current Web preview supplied by your project owner. On Web, use the main page, not `/original`.
- Have an internet connection for AI chat. Ask the project owner to confirm that AI access is configured before a presentation.
- Use only the fictional people and example messages below. Do not enter real patient details, passwords or payment information.
- No real account or phone-number verification is needed for this demo. Choose **Explore a demo**, not **Sign in** or **Say hello**.
- AI replies can vary. The examples below describe what to look for, not guaranteed word-for-word answers.

**Important:** Rumi is an AI companion, not a clinician. This demo does not diagnose, prescribe, change medicines, buy groceries, book rides, call anyone or send messages.

## What is AI, and what is a practice screen?

Keep these distinctions in mind as you present:

- **AI chat:** Sonnet 5.5 generates replies to your messages when the service is available.
- **Health questions:** AI chooses which sample records to read. The app—not the AI—calculates the numbers and draws the charts.
- **Calculated preview:** A chart built directly by the app without asking AI. Useful as a backup, but not proof that AI is connected.
- **Guided next step:** The app supplies the short invitation and follow-up question after a data answer. Those particular lines are not generated coaching.
- **Demo memory:** Goals you approve stay with that fictional member on that device or browser. They are not shared with a real health account or automatically synced between iPhone and Web.
- **Mock services and rehearsals:** Practice screens with sample content. A confirmation saves only a local demo change, not an action with a real company or care team.

## 1. Start with the primary demo member

1. On the welcome screen, tap **Explore a demo**.
2. Select **Primary demo member**.
3. Tap the **Start demo** button. On iPhone it reads **Start demo with Primary**.
4. Check that the conversation identifies this as a demo and shows the selected member.
5. For a fresh presentation, open the three-line menu, choose **Reset this demo member**, then confirm. This clears only that member’s local demo changes.

**Say:** “We’re using fictional records. The AI conversation is real when connected, but nothing here changes a real person’s care.”

**Navigation tip:** On iPhone, feature shortcuts appear below the conversation; scroll down to find them. Use the back button to return. On Web, open the three-line menu to find the same areas, then close the panel to return to chat.

## 2. Ask AI to explain the sample numbers

1. In the message box, paste this:

   > Show my A1c and eGFR side by side, and also my latest LDL.

2. Tap the up-arrow **Send** button.
3. **Web only:** If asked, read the AI-processing notice and choose **Allow AI processing**. Your message returns to the message box; press **Send again**.
4. Wait for the answer. A live request can take several seconds.
5. Look for chart cards and the label explaining that AI selected the request and the app calculated the facts.

**What to look for:**

- A1c, a blood-sugar measure: **9.0% → 8.8%**.
- eGFR, a kidney-function measure: **60 → 55 mL/min/1.73 m²**.
- Latest LDL, a cholesterol measure: **130 mg/dL**.
- Separate units and illustrative sample dates for each measure.
- A description of the values, not a diagnosis, a new treatment plan or a claim that one measure caused another to change.

**Say:** “I asked an ordinary-language question. AI worked out which records to read; the app worked out the numbers.”

**If AI is unavailable:** Show the error honestly. Open **Insights walkthrough** and use **Calculated preview · No AI request** to demonstrate the chart separately. Do not describe that preview as a successful AI reply.

## 3. Explore the chart, then ask a harder question

1. On a chart, switch between **30d**, **90d** and **All**.
2. Select **All** to see older sample results. A shorter window may contain few or no results; that is not necessarily an error.
3. On iPhone, drag across the chart to inspect a value and date. On Web, move the pointer over the chart, or use the date/value buttons in the disclosure below it.
4. Expand **Accessible values & sample dates** to read the values without relying on the chart line.
5. Return to chat and send:

   > Show my last 4 A1c results. If fewer exist, show only those available.

6. Look for only the available sample results. The primary member has **two**, not four.
7. Then send:

   > Compare my A1c with people like me and show my CGM time in range.

**What to look for:** Rumi should explain that the demo does not contain the required comparison group or continuous glucose monitor (CGM) data. It should not invent a ranking, extra readings or time-in-range percentage.

**Say:** “Knowing when information is missing matters just as much as producing a chart.”

**Shortcut:** The **Insights walkthrough** has buttons for these AI examples. They send real requests when connected; the calculated preview below them does not.

## 4. Move from numbers to a goal you choose

1. After a data answer, look for an invitation to discuss your next step.
2. Choose **Yes, talk about my goal** on iPhone, or **Yes, please** on Web.
3. Rumi asks what you would like to work toward. This short question is supplied by the app.
4. Send:

   > I want to prepare for my next PCP visit, but I feel overwhelmed. Help me choose one small next step.

   PCP means your regular primary care clinician.

5. To ask for practical help explicitly, send:

   > Please help me draft three questions for that visit, without recommending medication changes.

**What to look for:** A brief, supportive AI reply that helps prepare questions, acknowledges missing information and leaves medical decisions to the clinician. It should not change the supplied medicines or invent a personal treatment target.

**Say:** “The goal is to help someone prepare and choose, not tell them what treatment to take.”

## 5. Ask AI to propose a goal, then approve it yourself

1. Open **Saved goals & demo memory** on iPhone, or **Goals & memory** from the Web menu.
2. Turn on **Remember within this demo** on iPhone, or **Remember my demo goals** on Web.
3. Return to chat and send:

   > Please propose this as my demo goal for review: Write down three questions before my next PCP visit.

4. If AI offers a goal proposal, open it. On iPhone, look for **Review proposed goal**; on Web, the goals panel may open with the text filled in.
5. Read the exact wording before saving.
6. Confirm with **Confirm demo save** on iPhone or **Confirm local goal save** on Web. On Web, choose **Review exact goal** first if needed.
7. Reopen the goals screen and find your saved goal.
8. Return to chat and ask:

   > What demo goal did I just save?

**What to look for:** With demo memory enabled, AI can use that selected member’s saved goal in a new reply. A chat statement alone is not a completed save: check the goals screen.

**If AI does not offer the proposal:** Enter the same text manually in the goals screen, choose **Review exact goal**, then confirm. This demonstrates the review-and-save controls, not a successful AI proposal.

**Say:** “AI can suggest something to remember, but the person reviews the exact words and decides whether to save.”

Turning memory off stops future goal recall; it does not erase the visible conversation. Use reset to clear the member’s stored demo changes.

## 6. Show that Rumi keeps different people separate

1. Tap the member name near the top of the conversation. On Web you can also use **Switch demo member** in the menu.
2. Choose **Tom Becker**. On Web, press **Start demo** to return to chat.
3. If Tom has been used before, reset his demo for a clean run.
4. Send:

   > Show my steps and sleep side by side over the last 14 days.

5. Look for two sets of fictional daily observations, each with its own units: steps and hours of sleep.
6. Send:

   > I can manage a short lunch walk, but not a whole workout. Help me find a manageable next step.

7. Check Tom’s goals screen. The new PCP-visit goal you saved for the primary member should not be there.
8. Switch back to **Primary demo member**. Its conversation and saved goal should still be present unless you reset it.

**What to look for:** Tom’s own prevention-focused context, not Maria’s insulin story or the primary member’s lab history. Tom’s sample records do not come from a connected device.

**Say:** “The same AI adapts to a different fictional person without borrowing someone else’s history.”

## 7. Finish with a reviewed everyday task

This step demonstrates the app’s approval controls, not live social-media research or an AI purchase.

1. Open **Demo services · Instagram, Instacart & Uber** on iPhone, or **Rumi Works** from the Web menu.
2. Choose **Instagram** to see the mock recipe feed. These are sample recipes and invented creators, not real posts.
3. Choose a recipe and review saving it. Read the details and confirm the local save.
4. Open its pantry/grocery comparison. On iPhone the link opens the grocery review; on Web choose **Compare pantry & groceries**.
5. Read the recipe needs, fictional pantry and proposed missing ingredients. Pantry quantities differ between iPhone and Web, so use the amounts shown on your screen.
6. Choose the cart-review button. Check the exact items, then confirm the demo cart update.
7. Review the same recipe again. Items already covered by the pantry or cart should not be proposed again.

**Optional AI moment on iPhone:** In the mock recipe feed, tap **Discuss this sample with Rumi**. This asks AI about the seeded recipe; it does not fetch a real Instagram post. On either platform you can instead ask in chat:

> Help me think of one practical way to make weekday dinner easier. Don’t buy or order anything.

**Say:** “The person can see and approve the proposed change. This cart is local practice only—nothing is purchased.”

## Optional A. Talk to Rumi

### iPhone: live AI conversation, if the service connects

1. Select the member you want to use. Enable demo memory first if you want saved goals available in voice.
2. Tap the waveform button beside the message box, labelled **Talk to Rumi**.
3. Read the audio-processing notice. Tap **Start live conversation** and allow microphone access if you agree.
4. Wait until the screen says **Listening** before speaking. **Connecting** does not mean a conversation has started.
5. Say: “Help me prepare one question for my next visit.”
6. Listen for the reply and follow the live captions.
7. Try **Mute**, **Unmute**, then **End**.
8. If a transcript is available and demo memory is enabled, choose **Review transcript excerpt to remember**. Read it, then choose **Confirm local demo save** only if you want to keep it.

This uses **GPT-Realtime 2**, not Sonnet chat or the deployed ElevenLabs agent. It is an in-app AI conversation, not a telephone call. The retained excerpt is the displayed conversation text, not an AI-written summary. Voice cannot authorize purchases or save goals on its own.

### Web: dictate a message, not a live voice conversation

1. Tap the microphone button beside the message box.
2. Read the notice and enable **Allow browser speech processing** if you agree.
3. Tap **Start browser dictation** and allow microphone access.
4. Speak, then tap **Stop dictation**.
5. Review or edit the transcript.
6. Choose **Place in composer · Not sent**, then press **Send** to ask the chat AI.

Web dictation depends on browser support. It does not provide spoken AI replies. If either platform cannot connect or access the microphone, use text chat; do not present an error state as working voice.

## Optional B. Ask AI about a real public recipe

This is separate from the seeded mock recipe feed. Website access can fail; prepare a public recipe link before presenting.

### iPhone

1. Open **Browse public recipes with Rumi**.
2. Paste a complete public HTTPS address from MyPlate, Diabetes Food Hub, Budget Bytes or EatingWell.
3. Tap **Open source** and wait for readable source text.
4. Enable **Share this public page with AI** if you agree.
5. Ask: “Using this page only, what recipe information is available, and what is missing?”
6. Tap **Ask Rumi about this page**. Look for a reply grounded in the fetched page, not made-up details.
7. If Rumi proposes opening a link, read the destination before choosing **Approve opening this source**, or decline it.
8. To stop pending AI work, use **Take control** when shown. Choose **Resume · refresh source first** before asking again.

For ingredient extraction, open **Plan dinner & demo groceries**, then **Read a real recipe & calculate net-new groceries**. Paste a specific recipe link, read the notice explaining that website text goes to AI, and choose **Read recipe & compare pantry** only if you agree. Inspect the source and ingredients before reviewing any local cart update. Missing or incompatible quantities may block confirmation; do not guess around that warning.

### Web

1. Open **Rumi Works → Read a public recipe source**.
2. Paste an allowed public recipe address and choose **Read source**.
3. If text loads, enter a question and choose **Ask Rumi about this source**. Complete AI consent and press Send if prompted.
4. Use **Take over · Stop pending work** to pause, then **Refresh source & resume** to reload.

Web currently supports questions about readable source text, **not** the iPhone’s real-source ingredient extraction, proposed-link approval or source-to-cart journey. If a site blocks access, show the explanation or open the original page yourself. Do not claim Rumi read it successfully.

## Optional C. Explore Maria’s fictional care story

1. Switch to **Maria Alvarez**.
2. Ask: “Show my A1c and weight side by side.” Her sample A1c results are **8.6%, 8.2%, 7.8%**; weight moves from **216.7 lb to 208.7 lb**.
3. On iPhone, open **Explore health & care story**. On Web, open **Explore Maria’s DeRx story** in the menu.
4. Read the baseline and the notices about fictional prescriber orders. DeRx is the medication-management story in this demo.
5. Review a next fictional insulin event. First cancel it to show that review is not confirmation.
6. Open the review again and confirm only if you want to advance the local story.
7. Notice that the separate semaglutide story has not automatically advanced. It requires its own review.

**Say:** “These are deliberately selected fictional scenes, not AI prescribing or a medication-reduction rule. No real prescription changes.”

Never use these screens as instructions for a real person’s doses. The care story does not prove that any medicine caused a displayed change.

## Optional D. Rehearse a follow-up without sending it

1. On iPhone, open **Insights walkthrough → Refill, referral & lab follow-ups**. On Web, open **Care follow-ups** in the menu.
2. Choose **Prepare a question** under the refill reminder.
3. Read the exact question and selected answer, then choose **Cancel**.
4. Reopen it and choose **Confirm local rehearsal · Not sent** if you want a sample receipt.
5. Find the receipt in the local follow-up history.

These are seeded practice questions, not AI-generated active clinical orders. No pharmacy, clinician, lab or calendar is contacted. **Preview calls & messages** on iPhone and **Outreach rehearsal** on Web are also simulations, not live outreach.

## End or repeat the demo

1. End any voice session and stop pending AI or reader work.
2. Open the three-line menu.
3. Choose **Reset this demo member** and confirm if you want to clear the selected member’s conversation, saved goals, cart, receipts and story changes back to baseline. Other members are not reset.
4. Repeat for other members if needed.
5. Choose **Exit demo** to return to the welcome screen. Exiting alone does not reset saved demo changes.

## If something does not work

- **AI error or long wait:** Check the connection, use Stop if needed, then retry once. If unavailable, use the clearly labelled calculated preview and explain that live AI was not demonstrated. The app does not silently switch models.
- **No chart points in a short window:** Choose **All**. To see older points, ask for a trend rather than only the latest result.
- **Goal save is disabled:** Enable that member’s demo-memory permission and enter a goal. Review it before confirming.
- **Microphone error:** Check microphone permissions, or skip voice and type. Browser dictation is not supported everywhere.
- **Recipe source cannot load:** Try another allowed public source or use the seeded recipe feed. Do not label seeded content as live research.
- **Old conversation appears:** Reset that member before starting again. Demo state is retained locally when storage is available.
- **Sign-in or verification screen appears:** Go back to the welcome screen and choose **Explore a demo**. Real-account verification is separate and requires an explicitly authorized person; it is not part of this walkthrough.

## Presenter’s closing checklist

Mark only what you actually observed during your session:

- [ ] A live AI chat reply completed.
- [ ] A natural-language data request produced app-calculated facts and charts.
- [ ] Missing data was acknowledged rather than invented.
- [ ] A proposed goal was reviewed and saved locally.
- [ ] AI used the approved goal with demo memory enabled.
- [ ] Switching members kept their information separate.
- [ ] A local cart change was reviewed without a purchase.
- [ ] Optional voice or public-source research completed, or its limitation was explained.
- [ ] No real clinical, merchant, phone or messaging action was claimed.

**Build status is not a completed demo:** Native automated checks and the Web build have passed at earlier checkpoints, but complete live-AI, microphone, authenticated-account and cross-platform journeys remain unverified. This guide describes how to try the implemented flows; it is not a record that they all succeeded. See [PRODUCTION_HANDOFF.md](PRODUCTION_HANDOFF.md) for technical status and remaining work.
